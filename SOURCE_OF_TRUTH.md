# DUF Source-of-Truth Policy
## Authority Order
1. Malachi Dawkins's direct rulings
2. Approved and locked DUF master canon
3. Approved WiseMinds shared-continuity canon
4. Approved structured DUF Notion pages
5. Approved production documents
6. Google Drive and repository mirrors
7. Working drafts and archived exports
8. AI recommendations
9. External research and inspiration
## Conflict Rule
When two sources conflict:
- follow the higher-ranked source;
- document the conflict;
- do not silently merge incompatible claims;
- do not assume the newest file is approved;
- escalate unresolved matters to Malachi Dawkins.
## Repository Rule
A file does not become canon merely because it exists in GitHub.
Every canon-bearing file must state its status.
## Notion Rule
Notion is intended to be the living structured canon environment once its pages and links are verified.
## Google Drive Rule
Google Drive is intended for production files, offline documents, images, exports, layouts, and mirrored organization.
## Archive Rule
Markdown, HTML, PDF, or other exports from Notion are dated snapshots. They are not automatically the living source of truth.
## Obsidian Locks
Obsidian-locked subjects may be referenced by boundary and consequence but may not be defined or revealed without direct authorization from Malachi Dawkins.
